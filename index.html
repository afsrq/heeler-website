SET POLICY

Allowed. Needs sign-off. Never.

Management defines the company’s lists. The Never list applies to everyone, including managers. A trust level works within that policy; it doesn’t remove the company’s boundaries.

ASK FOR REVIEW

Anyone can send it to a manager.

Send to manager appears on every request. Add a note explaining your concern. The manager sees the requested action, who asked, and why, then allows or refuses it with a response.

KEEP THE RECORD

Know who decided what.

The team service records requests and decisions: who asked, who approved or refused, and what was said. Approval records stay on your company’s service rather than being sent to the makers of Heeler.

KEEP THE BOUNDARY

A missing manager isn’t a yes.

If the service cannot be reached, work that requires approval is not automatically allowed. Team voting is a management-authorized approval route, not a way around company policy. Teams controls help prevent mistakes within Heeler; they do not restrict someone running an agent outside it.

Your AI provider still processes the prompts you send to it under its own terms. Company-hosted approval records and AI processing are separate. Broader workspace sharing, budgets, and identity integrations remain roadmap items.

TEAM APPROVAL. BACKUP. CONTINUOUS MONITORING.

Trust your people.
Keep eyes on what ships.

Management doesn’t have to personally approve every push to stay in control of the process. Choose who can decide together, preserve a backup before their push, and choose how the result is monitored.

BEFORE THE DECISION

Equal voices. Chosen by management.

The manager selects the teammates authorized to vote on a push. Each selected member’s vote has equal weight. When the team’s approval requirement is met, the authorized push can proceed without a manager personally signing off.

BEFORE THE PUSH

Back it up first.

A push approved without a manager automatically creates a backup before it proceeds. The backup comes before the change—not after someone notices a problem.

AFTER THE PUSH

Keep watching the result.

Management chooses the monitoring AI or connects its own monitoring software. The selected monitoring follows performance changes and crashes after the push, with connected software reporting back to Heeler.

PRIVATE CONVERSATION MODE

Your organization holds the key.

We’re developing private communication built around a key created by each customer organization. The intended design lets an AI work with an encoded conversation while the organization retains the separate key used to interpret its meaning.

A familiar sentence can stand for something entirely different. “Is the grass greener on the other side?” might represent a private business question. The aim is to make the reply useful to the people holding the key without sharing that decoding key with the AI provider.

This describes the intended design, currently in development. Security validation is needed to establish what meaning the encoding conceals. Encoding alone does not establish that a provider cannot infer information, retain messages, or use submitted content for training; those protections also depend on the deployment and provider terms.
